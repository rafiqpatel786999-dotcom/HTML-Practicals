Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

  1. Ingredients
    1. 500g basmati rice
    2. 1 kg chicken or mutton
    3. 250g yogurt
    4. 2 large onions, thinly sliced
    5. 2 tomatoes, chopped
    6. 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
    7. 2–3 green chilies, slit
    8. Fresh coriander and mint leaves
    9. Garam masala, biryani masala, turmeric, red chili powder
    10. Saffron soaked in warm milk, ghee, salt, lemon
  2. Marinate the meat
    1. Combine meat with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt, lemon, chopped herbs and marinate for 2+ hours (overnight preferred).
  3. Parboil the rice
    1. Boil basmati with whole spices (cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaf) until about 70% cooked. Drain and keep warm.
  4. Fry onions and prepare aromatics
    1. Fry sliced onions until golden brown. Reserve some for garnish and use remaining for layering.
  5. Layering
    1. In a heavy-bottom pot, add ghee, spread marinated meat, sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander and a little saffron milk. Top with a layer of parboiled rice. Repeat if needed.
  6. Dum (slow cooking)
    1. Seal the pot (with dough or tight lid) and cook on very low heat for 30–45 minutes until meat is tender and rice is fully cooked.
  7. Rest and serve
    1. Let biryani rest 10 minutes, gently fluff layers, garnish with fried onions and serve with raita, salan and lemon wedges.
